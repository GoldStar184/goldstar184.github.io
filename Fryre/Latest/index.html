Project D20

Just remember, to roll the dice!

Allen and Desmond, 2 incredible creators in the digital industry, wanted to bring their love of Dungeons & Dragons into the Digital Age, hoping to spread the game into the modern era. After creating their project, they realized one of the biggest complaints they were receiving was a lack of consistent Dungeon Masters. So we helped them create artificial ones, based off of real human intelligence! We only finished the project in the last couple of years, and are still working with them to this day.

Digital Spark Solutions

Digital Spark came to us recently with a fantastic idea for a new project, tablets that could be used in elderly places. They would have all of the basic necessities, such as saved numbers, and a basic google search, but it would all be simplified, to provide an easier experience for those of us that didn’t have a large amount of technological experience. Although we had to part ways recently, back in November of 10', we were glad to support them in their works.
